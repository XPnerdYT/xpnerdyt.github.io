How It Works
  1. Item rests directly on the top sector right below the central camera.
  2. Camera identifies item type and rotates the internal 3-compartment drum.
  3. Door sector rotates around the central shaft to drop the item into place.
  4. If hazardous material is detected, the lid locks and rejects the item.
